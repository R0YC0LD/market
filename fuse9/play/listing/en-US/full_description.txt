FUSE9 is a Sudoku where nine cells are sealed.

Every cell is both a Sudoku cell and part of a minefield. You can't solve one without the other.

THE RULES
• Fill the 9×9 grid like Sudoku.
• Nine cells are seals: exactly one in every row, every column and every box.
• Open cells show, as dots, how many seals touch them.
• Each seal hides a different digit. Find the 4-seal, and every other 4 on the board is safe.

To open a cell, place its digit. To defuse a seal, hold the cell. Every move is checked as you make it: a wrong one is a strike, and it shows you the truth about that cell.

LOGIC, NOT LUCK
Every board is generated and then solved by a human-style solver before you see it. If it can't be finished with pure reasoning, it is thrown away. You never have to guess.

FEATURES
• Five difficulties, from Easy to Master
• A daily board, the same for everyone, with a streak
• Classic, Hardcore (one strike) and Zen (no limit) modes
• Hints that teach: first where to look, then why, then the move
• Notes, unlimited undo, automatic saving
• Paper and Graphite themes, high contrast, dots or numbers
• Original synthesized sounds and gentle haptics
• English and Turkish
