FUSE9 – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

FUSE9 is an offline puzzle game developed by R0YC0LD.

Data collected

FUSE9 does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, and uses no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party services.

Information stored on your device

Your game in progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. They never leave the device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

Only the vibration permission is used (for in-game haptic feedback).

Children

FUSE9 collects no data from anyone, including children.
